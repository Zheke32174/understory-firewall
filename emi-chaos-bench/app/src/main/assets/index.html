Passive spectral analysis of the mic input, in the spirit of tools like Praat or Melodyne: a coarse fundamental-pitch estimate plus the three loudest spectral peaks in the ranges voice formants typically occupy (F1 ≈300–900Hz, F2 ≈900–2500Hz, F3 ≈2–4kHz — the "singer's formant" band that lets a trained voice cut through without amplification). EFF's Rayhunter is the real, maintained IMSI-catcher detector — this app does not reimplement its detection, and Rayhunter's device-side web UI has no documented JSON API to poll directly.
